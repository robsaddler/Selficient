### Assessment of the name

- **Meaning.** It is self + efficient, which reads as "self-efficient" rather than "self-sufficient". Most people will still land on the intended meaning, and the Utrecht team reached the same word from the same idea.
- **The selfish echo.** The stressed syllable is "fish", so it is said sel-FISH-ent and the first six letters are one letter off "selfish". The echo is real but mild. It is memorable, and a product whose point is looking after your own household can carry it. A tagline that says "self-sufficient" fixes the meaning on first contact.
- **Spelling.** People will type selfficient, selfecient, selfishent. Register the obvious misspellings later if the brand sticks.
- **Verdict.** Workable, available on every UK-relevant TLD, and no conflicts found. Not a name to agonise over for an ASAP launch. Buy the three and move.

## Recommended actions

1. Register selficient.co.uk, selficient.uk and selficient.app today (about £10-20 a year each). Use the .app for the product and the .co.uk for the company site and email; .app enforces HTTPS, which suits a progressive web app.
2. Do the manual UK IPO and TMView search. If clear, file a UK trade mark for SELFICIENT in classes 9 (software) and 42 (SaaS), £170 for the first class and £50 for the second, once the name is final.
3. Ignore the .com unless the brand takes off; parked domains of this kind usually list at £1,000-3,000 and can be offered on later.
4. Reserve the handle on the app stores and social platforms when the developer accounts are opened.
