# Household resilience plan — 27 Rhodfa Glascoed

*Version 1, 29 October 2026. Evidence and prices are in [the review](../research/2026-10-29-household-resilience-review.md); context on the government campaign is in [the briefing](../research/2026-10-29-uk-be-ready-briefing.md).*

## 1. Goal

Four people can stay warm, fed, watered, lit, informed and in contact at home for **seven days** with no mains electricity, water, mobile or internet, with no injuries and no panic buying. Stretch goal: **a month** of food and an indefinite drinking-water capability. Where a purchase also cuts bills (rooftop solar) or removes a standing hazard (the tree, the decking), do it properly rather than minimally.

## 5. Decision register

Each decision lists the options, what you need to know to choose, and a default if you cannot find out.

### D1 — Rooftop solar and battery: go, go smaller, or wait

- **Options:** (a) 5.5-6.5 kWp plus 13.5 kWh with whole-home backup, £14-17k; (b) solar only with a small battery and an EPS socket, £9-12k; (c) wait.
- **You need:** I1 annual kWh (above 4,000 strongly favours (a)); I2 staying 10+ years (payback is 10-13 years); I3 roof survey confirming at least 11 panels on the front; I13 Flux status (reopened improves payback by 2-3 years); I14 loan and group-buying availability; three quotes with backup and scaffolding itemised.
- **Decision rule:** go with (a) if I1 is above 4,000 kWh, you expect to stay 10 years, and a quote lands at or under £17k with a gateway. Choose (b) if the roof takes fewer than 10 panels. Wait only if you may move within five years.
- **Deadline:** sign by end of January 2027 so the invoice beats the 31 March 2027 VAT change and the G99 lead time.
- **Default:** (a).

### D2 — Portable power station size

- **Options:** 1 kWh (~£470) or 2 kWh (~£850-1,000).
- **You need:** D1's outcome and timing.
- **Decision rule:** if solar with a backup gateway will be installed within 12 months, buy 1 kWh. Otherwise 2 kWh.
- **Default:** 2 kWh, since it is never wasted.

### D3 — The tall tree

- **Options:** no action; crown reduction or thinning (£400-1,500); felling and replanting further away.
- **You need:** I9 the arborist report; I7 ownership; I8 TPO status; I6 buildings policy wording.
- **Decision rule:** follow the arborist's written recommendation. If the tree is not yours, send the report with a letter to the owner asking for action within a stated period and copy your insurer. Do no work on a protected tree without council consent.
- **Default:** survey this week; nothing else until the report is in hand.

### D4 — How much cash

- **Options:** £500 or £1,000.
- **You need:** I6 the policy's cash limit.
- **Decision rule:** hold the policy limit in small notes; if you want £1,000 and the limit is lower, ask the insurer to raise it or accept the uninsured excess.
- **Default:** £500.

### D5 — Boiler on the power station

- **Options:** plug-and-socket on the spur; changeover switch; do nothing and rely on D1's gateway.
- **You need:** I4 boiler model and manual; an electrician's quote.
- **Decision rule:** if the manual allows a plug and D1 is more than six months away, do it. If D1 is imminent with a whole-home gateway, skip it.
- **Default:** do it; it is cheap and the gateway may be years away.

### D6 — Starlink Mini on standby

- **Options:** yes (£179 plus £9 a month, £55 in a month you use it); no.
- **You need:** I12 whether anyone must work or trade from home in an outage.
- **Decision rule:** yes if the business cannot run for two days without you online. Otherwise no; the radio and SMS cover family needs.
- **Default:** no.

### D7 — Gravity water filter

- **Options:** British Berkefeld (£160); none.
- **You need:** whether you want two-week-plus water capability, and I5 (a gas hob also lets you boil water as a fallback).
- **Decision rule:** yes if the month-of-food goal is real; the filter is what makes the month credible.
- **Default:** yes.

### D8 — Decking long-term

- **Options:** clear twice a year and fit mesh skirting; replace the timber skirt and nearest boards with composite; rebuild later.
- **You need:** I11 how it is built; whether boards lift easily.
- **Decision rule:** if the boards are screwed and the deck is above 150 mm off the ground, twice-yearly clearance plus mesh is enough. If nailed and low, plan to lift and mesh it next spring.
- **Default:** clearance plus mesh.

### D9 — Priority Services Register

- **Options:** register with NGED and Welsh Water; do not.
- **You need:** I10 children's ages and any medical needs. NGED lists families with young children as eligible.
- **Decision rule:** register if anyone qualifies; it costs nothing and brings doorstep water and earlier reconnection.
- **Default:** apply and let them decide.

### D10 — Tariff after solar

- **Options:** EDF Empower Fixed (10.36p overnight, 20.22p day); Octopus Flux if reopened; Agile with automation; Intelligent Octopus Go if an EV arrives.
- **You need:** I13; I2 EV plans; the installed battery brand (Octopus controls Fox, Sigenergy, Enphase, Tesla).
- **Decision rule:** Flux if open; otherwise EDF Empower plus the best standalone export tariff (E.ON 16.5p). Move to Intelligent Octopus Go the day an EV is on the drive.
- **Default:** EDF Empower Fixed.

### D11 — Heating fallback without the boiler

- **Options:** rely on D5 or D1; add a butane cabinet heater with CO alarm and ventilation rules; nothing beyond clothing and sleeping bags.
- **You need:** D5 outcome; whether anyone in the house is vulnerable to cold.
- **Decision rule:** if D5 or D1 is done, nothing more. Only buy a cabinet heater if neither is possible, and never run it overnight or in bedrooms.
- **Default:** nothing beyond D5.
