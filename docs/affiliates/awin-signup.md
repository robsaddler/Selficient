# Awin publisher sign-up: the answers

*13 November 2026. Worked out with Rob while he filled in the form. The application is not submitted: the form requires the site's address and Selficient has none yet. This is what to enter when it does.*

## Where it stands

- **Blocked on the URL.** Awin's form will not submit without the site's public address. Nothing else is outstanding on our side.
- **When the site is live**, open the form again and enter the answers below. Awin and each advertiser will open the address to review the site, so the landing page, a built plan and the terms and affiliate disclosure page need to be reachable when you submit.

## Promotional type

Tick one type, and make it the main one:

| Section | Tick | Main |
|---|---|---|
| Content | **Editorial Content** | Yes |
| Display | nothing | |
| Email | nothing | |
| Search | nothing | |

**Why Editorial Content.** Awin defines it as publishers who write editorial content on a specific topic, with links included where appropriate. Selficient writes a plan on one topic and puts a retailer's link beside the action that needs the item. It is also what an advertiser will expect to see when they open the site.

**Why not the others that look close:**

| Type | Why it is left unticked |
|---|---|
| Comparison Engine | The deals page lists the cheapest pick for each action, but it is a short curated list, not a search across many shops. Advertisers who see "comparison" expect a price-comparison site and may reject one that is not |
| Contextual Targeting | It reads like what we do, but it is a display-advertising type: technology that scans a page and serves banner creatives to match. Selficient shows no banners or creatives, only a text link chosen by hand |
| Discount Code, Cashback, Loyalty | They do not apply, and some retailers decline publishers who tick them |
| Everything under Display, Email and Search | No ad network, no newsletter, no bought search ads. The only email the site sends is sign-in codes |

A type can be added in the Awin profile later. If a newsletter or paid search is ever started, declare it first: some advertisers require the type to be declared before it is used.

## Sectors

All of the main ones are under **Retail & Shopping**, because that is where the catalogue's picks are sold.

| Sector | What it covers in the catalogue |
|---|---|
| **DIY** | Water butts, flood barriers, alarms, cartridge stoves, the hardware shops |
| **Home & Garden** | Water cans, bath liners, candles, fire boxes, garden kit |
| **Electronic Accessories** | Power banks, headtorches, lanterns, radios, router battery backups |
| **Gadgets** | Portable power stations and folding solar panels |
| **Sports Equipment** | Where Awin puts camping and outdoor shops: sleeping bags, flasks, stoves |
| **Department Stores** | The general retailers and supermarkets that stock several of the above |
| **Automotive** | The car inverter and the motoring shops |
| **Pets & Pet Care** | Pet food kept ahead |
| **Green (Eco friendly)** | Rooftop solar and batteries |

If the form limits how many can be chosen, keep the first five. Whether it has a limit is not known; the form was not seen from this side.

**Optional, depending on what is to be linked:**

| Sector | When to tick it |
|---|---|
| Baby & Toddler | For nappies and wipes. The catalogue carries no infant formula links on purpose, and ticking this does not change that |
| Internet Service Provider (Telcos & Services) | Only to apply to satellite or backup broadband programmes for the satellite internet action |
| Health & Beauty | Only if a chemist such as Boots is wanted for first-aid supplies |

**Left unticked, and why:**

| Sector | Why |
|---|---|
| Finance & Insurance (all) | The plan mentions insurance and cash but links to no financial product. Finance advertisers apply stricter compliance checks to publishers who declare the sector |
| Travel (all) | Nothing in the plan is travel |
| Pharmaceuticals | The plan sells no medicines: its medicine action sends a household to its own GP and pharmacy, and the catalogue carries no pick for it. Advertising prescription-only medicines to the public is illegal in the UK, and online pharmacy programmes vet publishers accordingly. First-aid kits are sold by general retailers and do not need the sector |

Sectors can be added later in the profile.
